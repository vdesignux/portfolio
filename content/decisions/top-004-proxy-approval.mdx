## Context

Packages cannot progress to booking without doctor approval. Doctors are practising clinicians and are frequently unresponsive within the window a conference allows — both inventory and airfare degrade while a package waits in Under Review. A package that cannot move is worse than one that moves with a record of why.

## Decision

Under Review to Active via travel user confirmation is a first-class transition, sitting beside Under Review to Active via doctor approval. The travel user must supply a written reason. The doctor is notified.

## Alternatives considered

**Timeout auto-approval.** Rejected. Silent, and attributable to no one.

**Admin-only proxy.** Not adopted. Adds a routing step at the moment speed is the entire justification.

**No proxy path.** Rejected. Leaves packages stranded and pushes the workaround offline — the failure mode the platform was built to end.

## Consequences

Approval as compliance evidence weakens on this path: the record shows that a travel user confirmed on the doctor's behalf, with a reason, not that the doctor agreed. The mandatory reason and the notification keep it accountable rather than invisible.

## Open questions

On the shipped Active screen the stepper renders "Under review" as complete whether the doctor approved or a travel user confirmed. The distinction lives only in the audit log. A marker on the package would have preserved it where people actually look — the clearest thing to change.
