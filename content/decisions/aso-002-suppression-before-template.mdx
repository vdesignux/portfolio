## Context

The requirements document described a five-step campaign flow: build a cohort, choose and edit a template, run a suppression and compliance check, review and send, then track delivery. Suppression checks the cohort against unsubscribes, hard bounces, blocked domains, do-not-contact entries and opt-outs.

## Decision

The suppression check runs before template selection rather than after it.
