## Context

Dashboards designed by the team shipped in Power BI, which has its own colour behaviour. There was no shared palette, and work shipped in inconsistent colour across projects.

## Decision

Derive the palette from Microsoft's own logo set, standardising every Microsoft blue into a single defined scale.

## Alternatives considered

**An independent brand palette.** Not adopted. Colour chosen against the platform rather than from it widens the gap between what is designed and what can be built.

## Consequences

Output sits natively inside the environment it renders in. The palette is a fidelity decision rather than an aesthetic one, and it set the pattern the typography decision followed.

Colour range is bounded by what the platform's own set contains, which is a real limit on expression and the intended trade.
