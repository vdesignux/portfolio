steps:
  - label: V1
    what: Standardised the dropdown pattern across filter types.
    why: Consistent, but still a flat list — it could not express a parenthesis.
  - label: V2
    what: Icons, conditional filtering, chip placement resolved.
    why: Conditions became readable; multiple values were still unhandled.
  - label: Design update 20/02
    date: 2026-02-20
    what: Required filters and the add-button flow.
    why: Adding a condition needed a defined entry point before grouping could be tried.
  - label: Design update 23/02
    date: 2026-02-23
    what: Multiple selected values per condition.
    why: Surfaced the ambiguity in mixing AND and OR across rows.
  - label: Design update 25/02
    date: 2026-02-25
    what: Error states for AND/OR.
    why: Error handling patched the ambiguity rather than removing it.
  - label: Design update 26/02
    date: 2026-02-26
    what: Horizontal layout accepted.
    why: Freed the vertical rhythm the parallel directions would need.
  - label: Design update 02/03
    date: 2026-03-02
    what: Three directions carried in parallel — inline, dropdown, nested.
    why: The remaining question was a trade-off, not a refinement.
rejected:
  - option: Nested filters
    rejectedBecause: Could express everything the engine could, but hid the query behind a modal and made structure something to parse rather than read.
outcome: Inline filters. Numbered rows replace nesting; the collapsed state returns the page to the results.
---

Seven versions across three weeks. The persuasive part is not the final builder — it is that the most expressive version was built, understood, and set aside for a stated reason.
